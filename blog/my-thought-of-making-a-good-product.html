一些做好一个产品的principel Some Principles for Making a Good Product

2026年8月3日 · 产品思考 Aug 3, 2026 · Product Thinking

把创作时的几条心法记下来，既是自省，也希望对你有启发。

  • 创作时，把自己当做使用它的用户；
  • 降低自己的知识认知，把自己当一个外部人员来使用产品；
  • 关注每一处每一角落，就像自己的家，不能散落垃圾；
  • 逐步变得更好，不可以期待第一个出品就是最完美；
  • 要关心作品的表达，是一个展示类型的、还是一个深度操作类型的，是面向最终用户的还是面向决策者的；

I wrote down a few principles I follow when creating, partly to reflect on, and partly in the hope that they may be useful to you.

  • When creating, put yourself in the shoes of the user who will actually use it;
  • Lower your own knowledge and experience, and approach the product as an outsider;
  • Care about every corner and detail, as if it were your own home — no litter scattered around;
  • Improve step by step; don't expect the very first version to be perfect;
  • Pay attention to how the work presents itself: is it a showcase type or a deep-operation type, is it aimed at end users or at decision makers;
