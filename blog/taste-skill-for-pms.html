Taste Skill 是一套给 Cursor / Claude Code / Codex / Gemini 等 AI 编码工具的开源审美规则包（SKILL.md），核心目标是解决 AI 生成界面千篇一律、廉价模板化（slop）的问题，给 AI 植入标准化、可落地的产品设计品味规则。

3. 优化要求：统一按钮权重、状态色仅用于业务流程提示、数字使用等宽字体、补充空数据/无权限兜底页面
4. 输出：审计问题清单 + 完整重构代码 + PM 验收检查清单

  • ⑤ 无占位假数据、无多余装饰性动效影响操作效率

四、PM 使用避坑指南（高频踩坑点）

FIG 05 · 五个高频坑 / Five common pitfalls

✗ 只喊「好看高级」

AI 回到通用模板 → 配 brief + 三参数

✗ B 端高动效

动效 >6、变化度 >7 干扰操作效率

✗ 跳过 Pre-Flight

异常页、移动端最易被省略

✗ 混淆个人偏好与产品调性

参数服务目标用户，不是个人喜好

✗ 替代成熟设计系统

适合原型与无专职设计师场景

  1. 不要只说「好看高级」，必须配业务 brief + 三参数。模糊描述会让 AI 回到通用模板；先讲用户、行业、产品定位，再明确三大档位参数，输出才贴合产品气质。
  2. B 端后台不要高动效、高布局变化。后台核心是高效操作，动效 >6、变化度 >7 会造成视觉干扰，降低运营效率。
  3. 已有成熟专业设计系统的项目，不建议完全依赖。适合作为辅助原型工具，最终落地以设计师设计系统为准；适合无专职设计师、快速迭代、营销页面场景。
  4. 不要跳过 Pre-Flight 预检直接交付开发。AI 容易省略异常页面、移动端适配，必须让 AI 输出自检报告，PM 核对完整流程再交付。
  5. 区分「个人审美偏好」和「产品目标调性」。Skill 的参数是服务目标用户，不是 PM 个人喜好：面向中老年用户降低布局变化、减少复杂动效；面向年轻创作者可拉高变化度与动效。

五、进阶：搭建团队专属产品 Taste 规范

  1. 在项目根目录维护自定义 SKILL.md，基于官方规则修改，写入公司品牌色、字体、交互文案规范；
  2. 沉淀不同产品线的固定参数模板（ToB 后台模板、C 端 APP 模板、官网模板），团队 PM 直接复用，保证全产品线设计品味统一；
  3. 把 Skill 预检清单纳入团队 PRD 交付标准，作为需求文档附带的视觉交互规范附件。

总结

Taste Skill 对产品经理的核心价值，是把模糊的「产品品味」转化为可标准化、可 AI 执行、可量化调控的设计规则，打通「需求 — 原型 — 前端 Demo — 验收」全链路，大幅降低视觉、交互、细节的沟通成本，让 PM 能快速落地有统一质感、贴合用户调性的产品界面，而非千篇一律的 AI 模板页面。

AI is getting better and better at writing code — but the aesthetics of the interfaces it generates often stay stuck at "centered cards, blue-purple gradients, and fake data everywhere." In the industry we call this slop: that cheap, template-driven taste. A product manager looks at an AI-generated prototype and instantly knows something is off — yet can't say exactly what.

The Taste Skill this article is about exists precisely to fix that. Let's define it clearly first:

Taste Skill is an open-source aesthetic rule pack (SKILL.md) for AI coding tools like Cursor / Claude Code / Codex / Gemini. Its core goal is to solve the "slop" problem — AI interfaces that all look the same and feel cheap — by giving AI standardized, executable product-design taste rules.

For a product manager, it is not a replacement for designers. It is a way to inject your product's tone, experience standards, and interaction constraints directly into AI, dramatically shortening the "requirement → prototype → high-fidelity frontend demo → development handoff" cycle, and unifying the team's visual & interaction floor.

4. PM Pitfall Guide (The High-Frequency Traps)

FIG 05 · Five common pitfalls / 五个高频坑

✗ Just say "premium"

AI falls back to templates → add brief + three dials

✗ High motion in B2B

Motion >6, variation >7 distracts from tasks

✗ Skip Pre-Flight

Edge pages & mobile are the first things AI omits

✗ Personal taste ≠ product tone

Dials serve target users, not the PM's own preference

✗ Replace mature design systems

Best for prototypes & teams without dedicated designers

  1. Don't just say "premium and beautiful" — always pair a business brief with the three dials. Vague descriptions push AI back to generic templates. First state the user, industry, and positioning, then set the three dial values explicitly, so the output matches the product's temperament.
  2. No high motion or high layout variation for B2B backends. A backend's core is efficient operations; motion >6 and variation >7 create visual noise and hurt operator efficiency.
  3. Don't fully rely on it for projects with a mature professional design system. Use it as an auxiliary prototyping tool; the shipped product follows the design system. It shines for teams without a dedicated designer, fast iteration, and marketing pages.
  4. Never skip the Pre-Flight check before handing off to development. AI tends to omit edge states and mobile adaptation. Always require a self-check report and confirm the full flows before handoff.
  5. Separate "personal aesthetic preference" from "product target tone." The dials serve your target users, not the PM's personal taste: for middle-aged/older users lower the variation and reduce complex motion; for young creators you can raise both.

5. Advanced: Build Your Team's Own Taste Spec

  1. Maintain a custom SKILL.md at the project root, forked from the official rules, and write in your company's brand colors, type, and interaction copy standards.
  2. Stock fixed parameter templates per product line (B2B backend template, consumer app template, marketing-site template) that the whole team reuses — keeping design taste consistent across all product lines.
  3. Add the skill's Pre-Flight checklist to your team's PRD delivery standard, as the visual/interaction-spec appendix attached to every requirements document.

Summary

Taste Skill's core value to a product manager is turning vague "product taste" into design rules that are standardized, AI-executable, and quantitatively controllable — connecting the whole "requirement → prototype → frontend demo → acceptance" chain, cutting the communication cost of visuals, interactions, and details. PMs can quickly ship interfaces with unified quality and a tone that fits their users, instead of yet another AI-template page.
